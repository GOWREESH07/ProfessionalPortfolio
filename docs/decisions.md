## 2. Director Casting & Cinematographic Translation
**Cast Director:** Robert Wise (*The Andromeda Strain*, 1971) & David Fincher (*Zodiac*, 2007; *The Social Network*, 2010)  
**Cinematographer Reference:** Richard H. Kline & Harris Savides  

### Why this pair?
- *The Andromeda Strain* (Robert Wise) provides the clinical, procedural discipline: split-frame analytical views, surgical annotations, monospace diagnostic indices, and zero decorative noise.
- *Zodiac* / *The Social Network* (David Fincher) provides modern technical velocity: locked camera coordinates, high-density informational rhythm, and deep graphite negative space punctuated by functional illumination.

### Mechanisms Translated to Code:
1. **Clinical Split-Composition (Wise)**:
   - Case studies abandon the generic centered card. Instead, they use structural multi-column grids where architectural diagrams occupy fixed spatial coordinates while code mechanisms and technical analysis scroll alongside them.
2. **Locked Camera & Machine Precision (Fincher)**:
   - Interaction is free of loose bounce or floaty spring physics. Motion uses crisp cubic-bezier timing (`cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)` and `ease-out`) with subtle coordinate tracking.
3. **Diagnostic Lighting & Negative Space**:
   - Backgrounds are deep obsidian and graphite (`#0a0b0d`, `#111215`). Lighting is practical: surfaces only brighten to reveal data density, active state, or topology pathways.
4. **Editorial Field Journal Typography**:
   - Space Grotesk for architectural headlines.
   - Inter for refined, high-legibility body prose.
   - IBM Plex Mono for telemetry metadata, protocol tags, and verification stamps.
5. **No Decorative Props or Copied Film Assets**:
   - Zero copyrighted imagery, film names, or sci-fi clichés. The interface is strictly a working software engineer's portfolio.
